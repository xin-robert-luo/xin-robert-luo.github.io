About

Research with practical reach

A career spanning information systems research, cybersecurity education, editorial leadership, international programs, and doctoral mentorship.

Xin (Robert) Luo is a Distinguished Professor at the University of New Mexico's Anderson School of Management, where he also serves as Endowed Creative Enterprise Chair and Endowed Dean's Professor of Research Excellence.

His scholarship examines how individuals and organizations make decisions in digital environments. Across cybersecurity, privacy, artificial intelligence, online platforms, and data-driven strategy, his work connects rigorous information systems research with consequential organizational and societal challenges.

Before joining UNM in 2008, he served on the faculty at Virginia State University. He earned his Ph.D. and M.S. in Management Information Systems from Mississippi State University, an MBA from the University of Louisiana, and a B.A. in International Business from Sichuan Normal University.

Research

Research interests

Three complementary streams investigate how digital technologies reshape security, decision-making, markets, and organizational performance.

01

Cybersecurity management and consumer privacy

Examining security behavior, privacy protection, insider threats, misinformation, cloud security, and the organizational consequences of security investments.

02

Strategic information systems and AI

Studying digital innovation, human-AI trust, big data capabilities, organizational resilience, and the strategic use of information systems for business decisions.

03

Online platforms, markets, and reviews

Investigating live-streaming commerce, online healthcare, digital marketplaces, blockchain-enabled ecosystems, metaverse engagement, and platform participation.
